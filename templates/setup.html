How to get API_ID and API_HASH
API_ID and API_HASH are not your Telegram password. They identify the Nexora CRM app to Telegram's servers — like a developer key. You get them once and they never change.
  1. Go to my.telegram.org/apps and log in with your Telegram phone number.
  2. Click API Development Tools → fill in the form:
    • App title: Nexora CRM (or anything)
    • Short name: nexoracrm
    • Platform: Other
  3. Copy your App api_id and App api_hash
  4. In cPanel File Manager, open .env in your app folder and add: 
    API_ID=your_api_id_here
API_HASH=your_api_hash_here
  5. In cPanel → Setup Python App → restart your app.
  6. Come back to this page — the Send OTP Code form will be enabled. Enter your phone number and log in directly here — no terminal needed.
Your Telegram session is saved on the server only. No password is ever stored. You can disconnect anytime from the button above.
